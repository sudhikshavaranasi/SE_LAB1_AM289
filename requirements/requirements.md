# Requirements Specification
## Food Bank Surplus Redistribution Platform

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow Donor Partners to post surplus food packages with quantity, food type, expiry time, and dietary tags. | High | Pass: A donor can successfully create and publish a food listing with all required details. Fail: The listing is published without required information. | Allows donors to make surplus edible food available to verified shelters before expiry. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall track the expiry time of each posted food batch and automatically deactivate batches after expiry. | High | Pass: An expired food batch is removed from the active claim board. Fail: An expired batch remains available for reservation. | Prevents shelters from reserving food that is no longer within its valid distribution period. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall notify eligible nearby shelters when a relevant surplus food batch is posted. | High | Pass: Verified shelters within the applicable notification area receive a notification. Fail: Eligible shelters do not receive the notification. | Helps redistribute perishable food quickly and reduces food wastage. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall allow Shelter Coordinators to reserve an available food batch and select a pickup window. | High | Pass: A shelter can reserve an available batch and receive a confirmed pickup window. Fail: The system allows multiple shelters to reserve the same batch. | Ensures that surplus food is allocated to a shelter in an organized manner. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall generate and validate a pickup verification code when a reserved food batch is collected. | High | Pass: A valid verification code confirms pickup and updates the reservation status. Fail: An invalid code confirms the pickup. | Provides a reliable way to confirm that the reserved food was actually collected. |
| NFR-001 | Non-Functional | The platform shall dispatch notifications to shelters within a 5 km radius promptly when high-quantity perishable food is listed. | High | Pass: Benchmarking confirms that notifications are delivered within the defined response target under simulated peak load. Fail: Notifications exceed the target response time. | Rapid notification is important because perishable food has a limited usable period. |
| NFR-002 | Non-Functional | The platform shall maintain reliable access to active food listings and reservation information during periods of high usage. | High | Pass: Simulated peak-load testing confirms that listings and reservations remain accessible without loss of confirmed reservation data. Fail: Active reservations or listings are lost or become inaccessible during peak usage. | Reliability is necessary to prevent failed pickups and loss of food redistribution opportunities. |
